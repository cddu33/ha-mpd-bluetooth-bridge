Synchronized groups

A group plays the same audio on several speakers, in sync, as one more media_player in Home Assistant (and in Music Assistant through its DLNA provider). If a speaker sounds late, play the test ticks and raise its offset until you hear a single tick. Offsets apply live and settle gradually over a few seconds.
